# Lane I2-paygate: a PAY never waits behind a melt

Branch `stage-3/int-pay-melt-gate`, on the Stage 3 integration head `9a20d30`. Contracts and
locked paths untouched: no contract request. Review record:
`docs/reviews/2026-09-25-pre-push-pay-melt-gate.md`. ADR 0012 has an amendment.

## What and why

The residuals lane's round-3 verifier found a fund-loss path. Core runs a wallet's operations
at one mint one at a time, and a melt holds that turn until the mint has paid the invoice (up
to 300 s). A video PAY at the same mint queued behind it. The worker gives `pay.build` 300 s
and the host cannot cancel a request, so the PAY was built after the worker had given up: the
viewer's proofs were swapped into P2PK sets locked to the seeder and the creator, never
delivered, with no refund.

Reproduced here before the fix: a melt held until its 300 s timeout, and a PAY asked for at
t = 0 made two P2PK swaps at t = 300 s.

The fix is a per-mint gate in the host's money plane:

- **A melt** marks its mint. PAY builds waiting their turn there are refused, the one in flight
  is waited for (at most the worker's deadline, then the melt is refused unmelted), then it
  melts, and the mark clears however it settles.
- **A PAY** is refused at once while its mint is marked: `rate-limited:`, before any wallet
  call, nothing spent, its blocks back in the session budget.
- **PAY builds at one mint take turns.** Core would serialise them anyway, and taking turns in
  the host makes the wait visible. At its turn a PAY re-checks the plane and its session.
- **The belt**: a PAY not at the wallet `PAY_BUILD_START_BY_MS` (105.6 s) after its request
  arrived is refused.
- **Every desktop melt goes through the gate**, because the plane's wallet is `GatedCashuWallet`.
  That covers the user's withdrawal (renderer `wallet.melt`) and an auto top-up's funding melt.
  The worker's `seeder.melt` is refused, so the host runs no earnings melt.
- **The numbers are shared** (`ipc/deadlines.ts`): the worker deadline (300 s), the melt timeout
  (300 s), the mint request timeout (30 s), and a model of a PAY build's worst host-side time
  (5 mint round trips × 30 s + 6 relay publishes × 7.4 s = 194.4 s), pinned by a test.
- **The worker** already treats a failed PAY as retryable: blocks stay owed, the session stays
  up, nothing reaches the seeder, so there is no ban. But it retried only at its next trigger,
  and none may come when every credit unit is held. After `rate-limited:` it now pays what is
  owed again on a backoff (2 s doubling to 30 s), so streaming pauses during a melt and resumes
  after it.

changed, with a comment: the boundaries test's module list gains `deadlines.ts`.

- `host/__tests__/pay-melt-gate.test.ts` (new, 9):
  - melt in flight → PAY refused and never built, another mint unaffected;
  - PAY in flight → the melt waits, and PAYs arriving or waiting are refused;
  - turns in arrival order;
  - the mark clears after a throw, a timeout, and two melts at once;
  - the belt;
  - the melt's wait bounded at the worker's deadline;
  - invalid durations refused;
  - `GateRefusal` on the wire;
  - no melt-like method on `CashuWallet` other than `melt`.
- `host/__tests__/money.test.ts` (+6), a real plane over the TestMint with a holding transport:
  - melt in flight → PAY refused at once, no request at the mint, no token events, balance
    unchanged; built after the melt;
  - PAY in flight → the melt's first request comes after both of the PAY's swaps;
  - **the verifier's scenario**: a melt held to its 300 s timeout and a PAY asked for meanwhile
    → no P2PK swap at or after the worker's deadline, and the next PAY is built;
  - the belt behind another PAY, with the budget returned;
  - session closed or wallet locked while waiting → refused at the turn;
  - the mark clears after a melt that throws.
- `host/__tests__/topup-host.test.ts` (+2), the whole host: the renderer's `wallet.melt` and an
  auto top-up's funding melt each wait for the PAY in flight at their mint, and PAYs there are

- `worker/__tests__/viewer-payer.test.ts` (+2):

  - the relay numbers match nostr-tools;
  - the worker entry sets no deadline of its own.
- `host/__tests__/mint-transport.test.ts` (+1): the transport's timeouts are the constants.

Residuals, in the review record:

- R1: other operations at the same mint (redeem, NUT-07, a top-up's mint, the settle loop) are
  not gated, so a PAY behind several on a slow mint could still overrun. A full fix needs core
  to take a deadline when it grants the turn, a locked-path change.
- R2: a NIP-46 bunker's latency is outside the model.
- R3: a withdrawal waits silently for a PAY in flight, up to 300 s at worst.
- R4: the belt starts at the host.
- R5: batching dips briefly after a melt.
- R6: a second wallet in `money.ts` would bypass the gate.
- R7: the host still cannot cancel a request.
