# Lane S3-f33: one seeder per block, credit per seeder window (F33, issue #8)

Branch `stage-3/f33-one-peer`, based on `c08c99f` (contracts v6). Issue #1 (security review F33;
Cameron's decision 2026-09-24: "request each range from a single seeder — no duplicates, seeders
paid fairly") and the issue #8 item "the credit pool is not sized per seeder window". No contract
change: the optional request is in `docs/contract-requests/S3-f33.md`. No IPC change. No new
dependency. ADR 0018 (`docs/decisions/0018-one-peer-routing.md`) records the design. An
independent review (verdict "fix first": one medium, seven low/info) was answered on the same
branch; see "After the independent review" below.

## What changed and why

### The finding

Hypercore can ask two or three seeders for the same block. The source is its hotswap
(`Replicator._updateHotswap`): any peer with spare capacity re-requests blocks already in flight
elsewhere. Each seeder counts what it sent, and must be paid for it (not paying window-cuts it).
So a raced block was paid twice: 29 deliveries for 24 blocks in the real-mint lane, and 55–67 for
48 on piped streams.

Separately, the desktop and the gateway kept ONE credit pool (4) for all seeders. That single
number:

- overran a seeder with a smaller window;
- starved one with a larger window;
- in practice, did not even hold at 4, because a raced loser answered after the cancel is dropped
  by hypercore, never paid, and still counted by the seeder.

### `OnePeerRouter` (`packages/seeder/src/net/one-peer.ts`, new; seeder portable entry)

Hypercore exposes no peer choice, so the router changes the two internals that decide it, on
each routed core:

1. **The hotswap queue** (`replicator.hotswaps`) becomes one that never races.
   - It yields a block only when its single request has stalled: at least `stallMs` old (default
     4 s; validated ≥ 50 ms) and its peer silent on that core for as long, or 4 × `stallMs` old
     whatever the peer delivers (after the review, IR3).
   - As the replacement request goes out, the stalled one is cancelled: a failover to ONE other
     peer.
   - A peer that stalled gets one request at a time until it delivers again.
2. **Each replication peer's `getMaxInflight()`**, read before every request hypercore makes to
   it, is capped at `inflight + budget(remote, core) − used(remote)`.
   - `used` counts in-flight requests, blocks being verified, and — for good — every request
     cancelled after it went out and every request that died with its channel.
   - Only the `UNCAPPED` symbol means "no cap" (it was `null` before the review, IR5); every other
     value that is not a finite number ≥ 1 asks nothing.

`refresh()` re-runs hypercore's scheduler when budgets grow. It is pinned to hypercore 11.35.3 by
`__tests__/one-peer-router.test.ts`, which checks:

- the version;
- the members, on real objects;
- the order of the `replicator.js` lines the router depends on;
- `PRIORITY.CANCELLED`.

`attachCore` throws `RoutingUnsupported` when the shape is not the pinned one, so nothing
downloads unrouted (fail closed). After the review, this also covers a peer already on the core
that lacks a pinned field. A released core is parked, never handed back to hypercore's scheduler
(IR1, IR6).

### `SeederCredit` (`packages/gateway/src/upstream/seeder-credit.ts`, new; `@sovit/gateway/upstream`)

This is the per-seeder budget the router enforces:

- **The budget** is `effectiveWindowBlocks(HELLO windowBlocks, manifest policy)` (clamped to
  1024) minus the blocks the seeder delivered that are not ACKed (`CreditSettler.owedBy`), minus
  the blocks it delivered that will never be paid.
  - Never-paid blocks are rejected PAYs and blocks owed when its `pay/1` went away. They are kept
    per Noise key after disconnects and inherited by the same signed HELLO pubkey under a new key.
- **The special cases:**
  - no HELLO yet: 0;
  - `pay/1` gone: 0;
  - no `pay/1` at all, or a core the settler does not own: 4 in flight at a time (the old pool
    size).
- **The pool.** The downloader's `CreditPool` follows the sum of the connected seeders' windows
  (`setLimit`), never below its construction size (the floor) and never above 1024.
- **The payer.** Its batch is half each seeder's window, and at once when what the seeder
  delivered fills its credit.

### Shared pieces changed (`packages/gateway/src/upstream/`)

- `CreditPool.setLimit` (`limit` is a getter now).
- `CreditSettler`:
  - `owedBy`, `linked`, `isPayable`;
  - `onChange(noiseHex, unpaid)`, which reports blocks settled without a payment.
- `UpstreamPayer`:
  - an optional `seederBatch`, with an at-cap trigger;
  - fixed a latent tail bug. The 2 s tail paid only the first of a quiet peer's scattered short
    runs, and the rest waited forever. The old small pool's pressure had masked it.

### Wiring

- **Desktop:** `ViewerPayer` owns a `SeederCredit` (`payer.seeders`) and routes every core it
  attaches. `close()` runs at worker shutdown, after `node.destroy()` (IR1).
  - `host.ts` marks a core attached only after the router accepted it, so a refused core stays
    refused on retry.
  - The fixture seeders take `windowBlocks` / `stallMs` options.
- **Gateway:** routes every upstream core (`watchCore`) and attaches `pay/1` peers to it.
  - `readUpstreamBlob`'s default lookahead is fixed at `creditBlocks − 1` (IR8; the lane first
    let it follow the pool).
  - `close()` releases the routes after `seeder.close()` (IR1).
  - `upstream.creditBlocks` is documented as the pool's floor.

## Residuals

The review has the detail.

- **Pinned internals.** A hypercore bump must re-verify the router, and the pin test fails first.
  A peer instance missing the pinned fields keeps hypercore's cap (logged; unreachable on
  11.35.3).
- **Withholding peers** delay one block at a time by 4 s each. Hotswap hid them by racing, at the
  price of paying duplicates.
- **Conservative debts.** They last for the life of the worker or gateway: cancelled after
  sending, lost with a connection, rejected, unpaid at a drop. An honest flaky seeder can lose its
  budget for the session. The contract request (the seeder's `outstanding` in `ACK`) would let
  the viewer resync.
- **Wall-clock steps.** A forward step of the clock larger than `stallMs` fails over everything in
  flight.
- **Unpaid after a drop.** Blocks delivered before a link dropped are debited, not paid on the next
  connection.
- **Pre-HELLO.** Requests to a `pay/1` peer wait for its HELLO: one round trip.
- **Real-mint test.** Routed after the review (see below).
- **ADR.** Written after the review as ADR 0018 (number assigned by the orchestrator).

## After the independent review (2026-09-25)

The reviewer confirmed the core claims: racing is closed, the cap sits where hypercore reads it,
late answers are never paid, and the budget fails closed. It found eight issues. All were checked
against the code and all are real. Seven are fixed, each with a test that fails without the fix
(mutation checks M29–M42). One, IR4 (low), is deferred. Detail:
`docs/reviews/2026-09-25-pre-push-f33-one-peer.md` § "Independent review".

- **IR1 (medium, fixed): shutdown could get the viewer banned.** Closing the router restored
  hypercore's own scheduler while connections were still open. The next block to land sent every
  held-back request to one seeder. Now:
  - **The router fails closed.** A released core is PARKED: every peer, and every peer that
    joins, is capped at 0 new requests, and the no-race queue stays until a router takes the core
    over.
  - **Shutdown order.** The desktop closes the payer after `node.destroy()`. The gateway releases
    its routes after `seeder.close()`.
- **IR2 (low, fixed).** The pubkey index follows a seeder's latest HELLO pubkey, so it can no
  longer grow under one Noise key.
- **IR3 (low, fixed).** A request fails over only when its peer has also been silent for
  `stallMs`, or when the request is 4 × `stallMs` old. Honest slow links no longer collect
  permanent debt.
- **IR4 (low, deferred).** A viewer restart forgets its debts. This predates the lane.
  Persisting permanent estimates would lock honest seeders out across runs. Needs the seeder's own
  count: the contract request now names this case.
- **IR5–IR8 (info, fixed):**
  - IR5: a dedicated `UNCAPPED` symbol replaces `null`.
  - IR6: peers without the pinned fields are refused at attach, and asked for nothing when they
    join later.
  - IR7: routes are keyed by replicator, and the image path no longer closes a playback core.
  - IR8: the gateway's default lookahead is fixed at `creditBlocks − 1`.
- **Orchestrator extras:**
  - ADR 0018 written.
  - The real-mint test routed through the production stack, with a labelled unrouted baseline:
    24/24 paid every run, against 25–27 unrouted.

## Fix round 2 (2026-09-25)

An independent verifier checked the fix pass and raised one finding (info, latent). It is fixed.
Detail: `docs/reviews/2026-09-25-pre-push-f33-one-peer.md` § "Fix round 2".

- **V1 (info, latent, fixed): a taken-over parked core had no failover ticker.** A parked core
  keeps its no-race queue and the blocks in flight it tracks. When a router took it over,
  `route()` reinstalled that queue but never armed the ticker, which starts only from the queue's
  `add()`. If nothing new was queued and the seeder holding a block withheld it, the block never
  failed over. `route()` now arms the ticker whenever the installed queue already tracks blocks.
  Production detaches only at shutdown, so nothing reached this path.
- **Test.** The router suite has 21 tests (+2, one `it.each`): a core is parked with a block in
  flight, then taken over by the same router or by another one, and the withheld block fails
  over to the other seeder.
  - Mutation M43 (no ticker on takeover) fails both cases: "still pending after 3000 ms".
  - The test also pins the known conservative double count of debt after a same-router detach
    and re-attach: 2, not 1.
- **Commands:**
  - Router suite: 21 passed, 3 runs in a row.
  - `npx vitest run packages/seeder packages/gateway packages/app-desktop/src/worker`: 502
    passed, 4 skipped.
  - `tsc -b --force`, eslint, prettier and `check:locked`: clean. `app-desktop` did not change.

In §0a, after the F33 entry:

> **F33 — fixed (`stage-3/f33-one-peer`, Cameron's decision 2026-09-24: one seeder per range).**
>
> - **Where duplicates came from.** Hypercore's hotswap (`Replicator._updateHotswap`) was the only
>   path that asks a second peer for a block already in flight. The router
>   (`packages/seeder/src/net/one-peer.ts`) replaces the replicator's hotswap queue with one that
>   yields a block only after its single request has stalled for 4 s, and cancels the stalled
>   request as the replacement goes out.
> - **Per-seeder caps.** It caps each replication peer's `getMaxInflight()` at that seeder's
>   credit, which `SeederCredit` computes: its effective window less what it delivered unpaid.
>   This also closes issue #8's "credit pool not sized per seeder window" and the old pool's leak:
>   a raced loser answered after its cancel was dropped by hypercore but counted by the seeder.
> - **Hypercore dependency.** The internals are pinned to 11.35.3 by tests. At runtime a
>   replicator of another shape is refused (fail closed).
> - **Seeders that misbehave.** A seeder that rejects PAYs while keeping the proofs is now bounded
>   to about one window of them. A `pay/1` peer that never sends its HELLO is asked for nothing.
> - **Independent review (2026-09-25).** A router closed while connections were still open used
>   to hand the cores back to hypercore's scheduler, and a seeder could then ban the viewer at
>   shutdown. Released cores are now parked (nothing more is asked), and connections close first.
>   A request fails over only when its peer is silent (or after 16 s). The real-mint lane runs
>   routed: 24 of 24 paid. Deferred: debts are per process, so a restarted viewer can overrun a
>   seeder that still counts the previous run (needs the seeder's count, contract request
>   `S3-f33`).
