## 1. One owed block, one connection (finding 1)

**Verified.** The reviewer's scenario as a test (`viewer-payer.test.ts`, "an OWED on a second
connection of the same seeder pubkey…"): node A (noise `aa…`) and node B (`bb…`) under one HELLO
pubkey, block 1 downloaded from A and waiting in A's batch (window 8, batches of 4), then B says
`PRICE` and `OWED [1,1]`. With the check removed (A1 below: the code at `aca2d6a`) the test fails
at its first assertion: B is handed block 1 as owed and its PAY built, while A still pays it
fresh — two PAYs for one block, as the reviewer measured.

**Fix.** `onOwed` collects the other noise keys whose current HELLO carries the same pubkey and
drops every recorded block one of them `holds` — pending (fresh or owed) or inside its PAY
awaiting the ACK. `holds` answers `false` for a closed connection (it will pay nothing). That
connection pays the block, and its PAY takes it out of the record; a block it drops unpaid stays
in the record for the seeder's next report (tested: A closes before paying; B's next connection
reports it and pays it, once).

- Also closed: an owed block being built on B while a third connection C of the pubkey reports it
  (before, C took it too; the record loses a block only after its PAY is built). Tested with a
  held owed build.
- **Per pubkey, not per link set.** The reviewer suggested `settler.owes(core, i)`, which spans
  every link of every seeder. A block downloaded from seeder Q now and left unpaid by an earlier
  run at seeder P is owed to both, and P must still be paid for it. Only links of the SAME pubkey
  share a record entry, so only they are compared.
- **Owed blocks per connection.** `ViewerPayer.owed` was keyed by pubkey and never cleared when a
  link went, so the next connection of the seeder looked as if it held a dropped link's owed
  blocks. It is keyed by Noise key now, cleared at the link's (current) detach, with its
  `pubkeys` entry.
- **Not done:** honouring only the first `OWED` per core per connection. With the check above a
  repeated or late `OWED` cannot make a block be paid twice (paid blocks leave the record; blocks
  pending anywhere under the pubkey are skipped); it can only make us pay blocks our record holds,
  which is the point of the record.

## 2. Transient never forgets (findings 2 and 7)

**Verified.** `upstream-payer.test.ts` and `viewer-payer.test.ts`: an owed PAY refused
`no-balance` (and `internal`, the tail file) for 45 s. With the old rule restored (G1 below) the
range is given up at ~30 s with no scope and `ViewerPayer` removes it from the record: the test
fails with `held()` empty where `[5]` is expected (the reviewer's symptom).

**Fix** (`UpstreamPayer.payFailed`, `payPending`):

- An owed range is never given up for a transient failure. It stays pending and owed on its
  connection, retried on the transient backoff (250 ms doubling to 4 s); once the streak has
  lasted `PAY_GIVE_UP_MS` over `MAX_PAY_FAILURES` attempts, on the deferred cadence (2 s doubling
  to 30 s) — never in a loop, never written off. Pinned in the test: 3 to 6 asks in the 90 s
  after that, then paid on the same connection within 30 s of the fault clearing.
- Only a final outcome (`forbidden`, `session-closed`) or terms it can never be paid at (none
  recorded, or asked above them) remove it from the record. A core the seeder stops pricing on
  this connection (it turns it free, or its price was forgotten under the new bound) drops owed
  blocks from this connection only, scope `connection`: before, that was a removal for good too
  (tested at both levels).
- **Separate streaks.** Owed blocks now back off on `owedFailures`, this connection's on
  `failures`. A kind waiting out its backoff is left out of the pass, and after a failure the pass
  goes on with the core (`continue`), so the other kind is tried at once. Without it, an owed range
  retried for minutes, lower in the core than this connection's blocks, was the first run of every
  pass and held them back (tested: owed 1 failing, fresh 5 paid in the same pass; then fresh
  failing, owed built). A kind that fails always has a streak in the future, so the pass cannot
  loop.
- The "seeder no longer reporting them" part of the decision: the record never pays a block the
  seeder does not report, and entries age out at 7 days (`UNPAID_TTL_MS`), as before.

## 3. One session per core (finding 3)

**Verified.** With `openCoreByKey` as at `aca2d6a` (S56 below): in `blob-store.test.ts` the
concurrent opens return different sessions, and in `images-paid-core` two `image.fetch` of one
profile image at once, with serving off, open two sessions (`expected 2 to be 1`) — the
reviewer's finding.

**Fix** (`BlobStore`): an open in flight is shared by name (`key:<hex>` for a key), so concurrent
callers get one session and one `onCoreOpened` (one gate); `closeCoreByKey` then closes the only
session. An open that finds the core registered in the other form meanwhile (a name open racing
a key open) closes its own session and returns the registered one — under its NAME when the
named open finishes second, so a core this store writes to is never closed as a replica. An open
whose `ready()` fails closes its session, and a retry opens afresh. Tested at both levels.

## 4. One bounded answer for free (finding 4)

`UpstreamPayer` takes `servesFree` (the gateway and the worker pass `SeederCredit.servesFree`,
the settler's own answer, bounded at 256 per connection). Its own set, used without the option, is
bounded the same way (`remember`: the least recently said first), and its per-connection price
map at `MAX_PRICED_CORES_PER_SEEDER = 1024`. Tested: with `servesFree`, a core the settler forgot
is pended and paid; without it, the 257th free word evicts the first; the 1025th price evicts the
first (its owed blocks are no longer taken).

## 5. Free requests share the window while in flight (finding 5)

The reviewer's two options: the seeder serving free what was asked while free, or the viewer
capping free requests. The seeder side cannot tell a request made while it said free from one
made after: hypercore reports uploads, not request arrivals, and a gateway that pays that core
counts the same blocks as sold (serving them free after the priced `PRICE` would make it pay for
uncounted blocks, refused `range-not-uploaded`). So the viewer side:

- `OnePeerRouterOptions.room(remote)`: what the seeder may still count over every core.
  `SeederCredit.roomOf` = its bare HELLO window (a core's widened window is never smaller) less
  what it counts (owed on the link, and from before). Given, a free request fits under
  `room − used − (free requests out)`, and a counted request under
  `budget − used − (free requests out)`. Junk or a throw is 0 (fail closed).
- The free requests are still never `used`, `inflight` or `debt`: landed, cancelled or lost they
  leave nothing owed (P2's rule kept).
- **Cost:** while a seeder's window is full of video, image requests to it wait for room (another
  free seeder may serve them). Recorded in ADR 0015.
- Tests: the router on real Corestores (2 counted requests out → the free core may take 2, not 4;
  2 free requests out → the video may ask nothing more); `SeederCredit` with fake replication
  peers (the wiring and `roomOf`).

## 6. Sold survives a restart (finding 6)

**Verified.** `images-paid-core`, new last tests: the video played in full, plus a simulated
upload (a blob stored through the worker's seeder and priced as `upload()` does); the worker is
restarted with seeding and image serving on, and an attacker's thumbnail names each. With the
policies not loaded back (S8 below, the state at `aca2d6a`) the test fails: the read is not
refused (the reviewer saw it return the video, `isFreeCore` true).

**Fix.** `CorePolicyStore` (`packages/seeder/src/store/core-policies.ts`):
`<dataDir>/core-policies.json`, written in the background (serialised, atomic, a burst coalesced
into one write), awaited by `Seeder.close`, loaded at `Seeder.create`, checked entry by entry
(a malformed entry dropped and warned, an unreadable file logged and started empty), at most
16 384 (least recently set first). `Seeder` loads them back into its per-core map, so:

- `setFreeCore` refuses such a core (its existing rule: a core with a policy is never free);
- the worker's `soldCore` sees it (`corePolicyMap()`), and `image.fetch` refuses it `forbidden`;
- the seeder prices it again as before the restart (read from the code, not measured: after a
  restart such a core had no terms at all on the worker's seeder, which has no default policy —
  no `PRICE`, and a PAY for it could not be verified).

The reviewer's other option (never mark a core free when it is already on disk) would also have
refused honest image replicas cached by an earlier run.

## 8. Write-ahead removal (finding 8)

**Verified** (`viewer-payer.test.ts`, "…leaves the record's FILE before it is sent"): with the
removal left to the next batch (A2 below, as at `aca2d6a`) the test fails — the file still lists
the blocks when their PAY is handed to the wire, so a crash there brings them back.

**Fix.** `UnpaidRecord` tracks the blocks added since its last write that were not in the record
at it (`unsaved`). `removeNow` (used for every built PAY, owed or fresh) forgets the blocks and
writes the record synchronously when any removed block was in the file; blocks received and paid
within one batch never were, and cost no write (tested: zero writes). A block re-recorded under
another session after a write counts as in the file. A failed write returns `false` and is logged
(counts only); the PAY still goes out — its proofs are built — and the next batch retries.

## 9. Our own profile core (finding 9)

`BlobStore.openCore(name, { beforeOpened })` runs the hook when THIS call opened the core, after
ready and before `onCoreOpened` (the upload gate and the terms said to already-paired peers).
`Seeder.openCore(name, { free: true })` marks the core free there (never a core with a policy),
then `setFreeCore` again for an already-open core. The worker's `ownProfile` opens this way. Test:
the free mark is already set when the gate is attached.

**Blast radius** (non-test callers at `HEAD`): `UpstreamPayer` — the gateway and the worker (both
wired with `servesFree`); `OnePeerRouter.cap` — only through `SeederCredit` (gateway, worker);
`BlobStore.openCore`/`openCoreByKey` — the daemon (named cores at start), the worker's play and
image paths, the dev fixtures, the store's own puts; `Seeder.setCorePolicy` — the worker (play,
upload, own profile) and the dev fixtures; the gateway and the daemon never call it, so their
policy file stays empty.

**Removed code.** Nothing security-relevant was removed. `payFailed`'s "give up after
`PAY_GIVE_UP_MS`" now applies to this connection's blocks only (fix round 5's rule, kept for
them); `giveUpOwed` without scope still runs for terms that can never be paid.

**Adversarial pass** — attacker: a seeder operator (one or several nodes, one HELLO key), a
publisher of hostile manifests, a peer flooding pay/1 frames:

- *Two nodes, one key, report a block pending on the other.* Skipped; paid once on the holding
  link. *Hold a block pending to block the other node's payment?* A pending block is paid within
  the batch or the 2 s tail timer on its own link; only the node that delivered it is paid.
- *Make an owed range retry for ever?* Only blocks our own record holds, one ask per ≤ 30 s per
  core per connection past the first 30 s; each ask is one host `pay.build` (two tail-file writes
  when it is refused). Bounded by the record (1024 blocks per seeder).
- *Flood PRICE frames.* Free words and prices are bounded per connection; the oldest evicted. A
  seeder that evicts its own lowered price is then paid at its HELLO price (≤ the manifest) and
  may refuse it — its own doing; it can always refuse and keep a PAY's proofs.
- *Say free for 257 cores.* The payer now follows the settler: the evicted core's blocks are paid
  at the manifest terms, as for any sold core.
- *Hostile manifest naming a core we should keep free.* Our own profile core: refused and cleaned
  (D1). Another creator's profile core: sold for us from then on (D2).

## Sharp edges

| API | Edge | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| `OnePeerRouterOptions.room` | Optional: a router given `free` without `room` has P2's behaviour (free requests by budget alone) | Low: `SeederCredit` is the only composer and passes it; documented on the option. Zero, negative, `NaN`, `±Infinity` and a throw all give 0 (fail closed) |
| `UpstreamPayerOptions.servesFree` | Absent: the payer's own bounded set, which a flood can make disagree with the settler | Low: both compositions pass it. A throw is `false` (owed: the safe side) |
